Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Introduction

YunaMoriFit ("we", "our", or "us") operates a content management platform that enables creators to schedule and publish lifestyle and fitness content to TikTok. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application and services.

Information We Collect

When you use YunaMoriFit, we may collect the following information:

  • TikTok Account Information: When you log in with TikTok, we receive your TikTok username, display name, and profile picture as authorized through TikTok's Login Kit.
  • Content Data: Videos, captions, and scheduling preferences you provide for publishing to TikTok.
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics about how you interact with our platform.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Authenticate your identity via TikTok Login Kit.
  • Publish content to your TikTok account on your behalf, as you direct.
  • Schedule and manage your content queue.
  • Improve and maintain our platform.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With TikTok, as necessary to publish content on your behalf through their API.
  • If required by law or legal process.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely and we implement reasonable security measures to protect against unauthorized access. We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. You may also revoke YunaMoriFit's access to your TikTok account through TikTok's settings.

Children's Privacy

YunaMoriFit is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
